I would think carefully about who your users are, and to what extent this might be meeting their information needs. There is a large gravitational pull toward interactive dashboards, but not a lot of data on how well they truly work to convey information or influence decision making. Is this for the lay public? A policy maker?  What kind of policies? What kinds of public health functions?

Some of these people have quantitative skills and information literacy and others do not. Which group is the tool for? Is there a risk of building something for the lowest common denominator?

How will you integrate these two data sources (Oral health outcome data and Demographic and socioeconomic data) in the dashboard?

What makes a dashboard trustworth?
