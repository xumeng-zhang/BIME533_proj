KEY FINDINGS: NHANES 2009-2010 Oral Health Disparities
======================================================================

1. Racial/Ethnic Disparity in Untreated Decay: Mexican American has 1.9x higher prevalence (21.4%) compared to Other Race (11.1%)

2. Education Gradient in Oral Health: College graduate have 7.2 more teeth on average (23.3) compared to Less than HS (16.1)

3. Poverty and Complete Tooth Loss: Below poverty have 1.4x higher rate of edentulism (26.0%) vs Above poverty (18.1%)

4. Overall Oral Health Burden: 14.3% have untreated tooth decay and 21.0% have lost all their teeth
